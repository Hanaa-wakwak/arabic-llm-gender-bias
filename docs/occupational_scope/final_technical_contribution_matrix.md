## Final Technical Claim

The thesis contributes a robustness-oriented Arabic occupational gender-bias evaluation suite. It does not only measure model-level bias; it also evaluates whether the measurement remains stable across occupation coverage, templates, semantic frames, dialects, stereotype balancing, and explicit job-title contexts.
